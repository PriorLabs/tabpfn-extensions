`bayesian_optimization.propose_next_point` now maximises EI with a gradient-free sampled optimiser (2048 uniform candidates, then three rounds of shrinking Gaussian refinement around the top 16) and works with an ordinary `TabPFNRegressor`. Gradient ascent on EI is still available via `n_refine_steps > 0`, which needs `differentiable_input=True`, but is off by default.
