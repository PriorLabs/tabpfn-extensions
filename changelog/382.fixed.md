`interpretability.class_vote`'s docstring now records that the vote reproduces `predict_proba` exactly only in full precision: under bf16 autocast (which TabPFN enables by default on CPUs with native bf16 support) or fp16 on GPU, the decoder head's log/softmax round trip costs ~1e-2 relative accuracy. The test covering that identity pins `inference_precision=torch.float32` accordingly, instead of passing or failing depending on the CPU a CI runner lands on.
